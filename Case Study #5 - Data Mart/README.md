## Case Study #5 - Data Mart

### Description
>This is the FIFTH of eight challenges I will be solving over the next eight weeks.

### Problem Statement
> Data Mart is Danny’s latest business venture. After managing international operations for his online supermarket specializing in fresh produce, Danny is now seeking analytical support to evaluate his sales performance.

> In June 2020, Data Mart introduced a major shift in its supply chain: all products began using sustainable packaging methods at every stage — from the farm all the way to the customer. This sustainability-focused change may have influenced customer behavior and, consequently, sales performance.

> Danny wants to quantify the impact of this change and understand how it affected different areas of the business — including platforms, regions, segments, and customer types. He is also looking for insights to help guide future sustainability updates in a way that minimizes negative impacts on sales.

> This case is inspired by a real-life scenario in the Australian retail market, where supermarkets stopped providing free plastic bags. As expected, many customers adjusted their shopping behavior as a result of that decision.

> Analyzing key events that significantly impact business or engagement metrics is a core skill in data analytics. This case offers a valuable opportunity to build that skillset and apply practical solutions to real-world business challenges.
